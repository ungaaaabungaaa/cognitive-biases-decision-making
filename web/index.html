Cognitive Bias Lab

Seven classic experiments. Ten minutes. Find out where your intuition misleads you, and what to do about it.

How this works

You will face seven short decision problems drawn from landmark studies in judgement and decision-making (Tversky & Kahneman, Wason, Arkes & Blumer, and others). Answer with your honest first reaction: there is no benefit to over-thinking, and the point is to see what your fast, intuitive System 1 does.

After each task you get a debrief that explains the bias, how the original participants answered, and a concrete technique for avoiding it. At the end you get a personal scorecard and can download your responses as a CSV compatible with the cogbias analyze command in this repository.

Nothing is uploaded. Everything runs in your browser.

Part of cognitive-biases-decision-making. Task materials are paraphrased from the cited studies for educational use.
